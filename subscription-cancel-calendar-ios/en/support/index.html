Subscription Manager Support

"Subscription Manager" is an iOS app for organizing subscription renewal dates and cancellation decision dates, and for using local notifications to make review timing easier to notice.

What You Can Do

  • Manage subscription names, categories, amounts, currencies, notes, and status
  • Record renewal dates and cancellation decision dates
  • Use local notifications based on notification settings
  • Organize timing for subscription review

Contact notes

When contacting us, please include the iOS version you are using and the situation in which the issue occurred.

Usage notes

This app is a tool for supporting management of renewal dates and cancellation decision dates. Please verify registered content and final contract decisions yourself.

FAQ

  1. Is data synced?

    No. This app does not provide accounts or cloud sync. Data is stored only on your device.

  2. What should I check if notifications do not arrive?

    Check whether notifications are allowed for this app in iOS Settings, and whether notification settings or Focus modes are affecting delivery.

  3. Where is registered data stored?

    It is stored only on your device and is not sent to the developer's server.

  4. Can data be transferred when changing devices?

    This app does not provide its own data migration feature. Transfer depends on iOS backup or migration methods and is not guaranteed.

  5. Does the app cancel subscriptions for me?

    No. This app does not make cancellation decisions or perform cancellation procedures. Please follow each service's official process.

  6. Can it convert USD and JPY?

    No. This app does not perform currency conversion. Amount and currency management is for record keeping and is not financial advice.
