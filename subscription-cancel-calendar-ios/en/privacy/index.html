Subscription Manager Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Subscription Manager" handles user information.

1. Information Collected

This app does not collect personal information. The following information entered in the app is stored only on the user's device.

  • Subscription names
  • Categories
  • Renewal dates
  • Cancellation decision dates
  • Notification settings
  • Amounts
  • Currencies
  • Notes
  • Status

2. Notifications

This app uses local notifications only when the user allows notifications, to help manage renewal dates and cancellation decision dates.

Local notifications are processed on the device. Notification-related information is not sent to an external server.

3. No External Transmission

This app does not send registered data to the developer's server or other external services.

This app does not use external APIs, advertising, login features, analytics SDKs, or server-dependent features.

4. No Third-Party Sharing

This app does not provide user information to third parties.

Information entered by the user is not shared with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

5. No Tracking

This app does not track users.

This app does not use advertising identifiers or third-party tracking tools to track user behavior.

6. Disclaimer

This app is a tool for supporting management of subscription renewal dates and cancellation decision dates.

This app does not perform subscription cancellations.

This app does not provide currency conversion, financial advice, contract advice, or other professional advice.

Users are responsible for managing the accuracy, recency, and legality of registered content.
