About "Shopping Profile"

Shopping Profile lets you keep your own and your family's sizes, favorite brands, and preferences in one place, so you can check them right before shopping. A shared profile can be reviewed on an import confirmation screen before it is saved, and it will never overwrite your own profile.

Open the app

If Shopping Profile is installed, it should open automatically and show the import confirmation screen. If it doesn't open, get the app from the App Store below, then open this share link again.

About this share link

This link contains the shared profile's content. Anyone who has the link may be able to view it, so please don't open a share link you weren't expecting.

Shared content is never sent to or stored on the developer's servers. Both this page and the app process the link's content only on your device.
