Shopping Profile Support

Shopping Profile is an app for saving your own and your family members' sizes, brands, and preferences so you can check them quickly before shopping.

What you can do with the app

  • Create shopping profiles for yourself, family members, and others.
  • Save clothing, pants, shoe, ring, hat, glasses, underwear, and custom size records.
  • Save favorite brands and notes about fit.
  • Save favorite colors, disliked colors, materials to avoid, and gift notes.
  • Copy saved information, share your own profile as a file, and import profiles you receive.
  • Use optional app lock and delete all local data from Settings.

Data storage

Information you enter is stored on your device. The app does not use accounts, login, cloud sync, or external API integrations.

You can delete saved information from each edit screen or from Settings.

Sharing

Only your own profile can be shared as a file through an explicit user action. Items marked as not shared are not included in the shared file.

Profiles you receive are saved as family or other profiles. Your own profile is not overwritten.

Privacy Policy

Please review the Privacy Policy.

FAQ

  • Your data is not sent to the developer's server.
  • If you delete the app, data stored locally on your device is also deleted.
  • When you send or paste shared files through another service, that service handles the data according to its own terms and privacy policy.
