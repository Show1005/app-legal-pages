Voice Stream Memo / 声メモ Support

"Voice Stream Memo" (Japanese name: "声メモ") is an app where voice input from iPhone or Apple Watch is converted into text memos and saved.

iPhone Basic Usage

  1. Tap Start.
  2. Speak.
  3. Tap Stop.
  4. Open History to view, copy, or delete saved memos.

Apple Watch Basic Usage

  1. Open Voice Stream Memo / 声メモ on Apple Watch.
  2. Tap the text field.
  3. Use the system dictation/text input to enter a memo.
  4. Tap Save.
  5. The memo is sent to the paired iPhone and appears in the iPhone app History.

Permissions

  • On iPhone, Microphone permission is required for voice input.
  • On iPhone, Speech Recognition permission is required for transcription.
  • On Apple Watch, memo text is entered through the system dictation/text input UI provided by watchOS.

iPhone Troubleshooting

  • If transcription does not start on iPhone, check the Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions for this app in iOS Settings.
  • If the text is not accurate, try speaking clearly and check your device's language settings and surrounding noise.
  • Empty recordings (no speech transcribed) are not saved to History.
  • If a session is interrupted, a draft containing the text transcribed so far may appear as "Draft" in History, so your text is not lost.

Apple Watch Troubleshooting

  • Apple Watch use requires a paired iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Delivery to iPhone may not be immediate; it depends on WatchConnectivity and system delivery.
  • If the memo does not appear immediately, open the iPhone app later and check History again.
  • The Apple Watch app does not provide continuous streaming transcription like the iPhone app.
  • The Apple Watch app uses system dictation/text input; accuracy depends on watchOS dictation, device language, surrounding noise, and environment.
  • Empty text is not saved.
