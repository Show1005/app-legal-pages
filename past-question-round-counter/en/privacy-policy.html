Past Exam Round Counter Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Past Exam Round Counter" handles user information.

1. Information Collected

This app does not collect personal information. The following information entered in the app is stored only on the user's device.

  • Material names
  • Section names
  • Number of rounds
  • Registration dates
  • Correct answer counts
  • Total question counts
  • Notes

2. Data Transmission

Data registered in this app is not sent to the developer's server.

User data is managed only on the user's own device.

3. External Services

This app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking tools, login features, cloud sync, or external APIs unless otherwise noted above.

4. App Data

This app's data is stored on your device.

If you delete the app, data stored on your device may also be deleted.

5. Disclaimer

This app is a tool for supporting records of past exam or workbook rounds and accuracy checks.

This app does not guarantee exam success, better grades, or learning outcomes.
