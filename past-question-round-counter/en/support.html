Past Exam Round Counter Support

"Past Exam Round Counter" is an iOS app for recording study rounds, section names, correct answers, total questions, and notes for past exam questions and workbooks.

What You Can Do

  • Manage study materials
  • Record rounds by section
  • Calculate accuracy from correct answers and total questions
  • Check the latest status for each material
  • Review accuracy trends

FAQ

  1. Where is my data stored?

    It is stored only on your device.

  2. Can the developer see my material names or study records?

    No. The developer cannot view your registered data.

  3. Do I have to enter correct answers and total questions?

    No. You can record only the number of rounds. Accuracy is shown only when both correct answers and total questions are entered.

  4. What should I enter as a section name?

    Enter a name that will be easy to review later, such as a year, chapter, topic, or exam session.

  5. Is cloud sync available?

    No. This app stores data only on your device.

  6. Does this app guarantee exam success or better grades?

    No. This app supports study record keeping and does not guarantee exam results.
