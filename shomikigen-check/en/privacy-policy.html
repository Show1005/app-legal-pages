Expiry Date Check Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Expiry Date Check" handles user information.

1. Information Collected

This app does not collect personal information. The following information entered in the app is stored only on the user's device.

  • Food names
  • Opening dates
  • Best-before dates
  • Notes
  • Notification settings

2. Data Transmission

Data registered in this app is not sent to the developer's server.

User data is managed only on the user's own device.

3. External Services

This app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking tools, login features, cloud sync, or external APIs unless otherwise noted above.

4. Notifications

This app uses local notifications only when you enable notifications.

Local notifications are processed on your device. User data is not sent to a developer server for notifications.

5. Disclaimer

This app is a support tool for recording and checking food opening dates and best-before dates.

This app does not guarantee food safety, freshness, or whether food is suitable to eat.

Please check food labels, storage conditions, smell, appearance, and other factors, and use your own judgment.
