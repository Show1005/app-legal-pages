Expiry Date Check Support

"Expiry Date Check" is an iOS app for recording food names, optional opening dates, optional best-before dates, notes, and local notifications.

What You Can Do

  • Manage food names
  • Record opening dates
  • Record best-before dates
  • Save notes
  • Use local notifications as reminders

FAQ

  1. Where is my data stored?

    It is stored only on your device.

  2. Can the developer see my registered food data?

    No. The developer cannot view the data you register.

  3. Why are notifications not appearing?

    You may need to allow notifications for this app in iOS Settings. Please check the notification settings in the Settings app.

  4. Can this app determine whether food is safe to eat?

    No. This app is a tool for recording and checking information. It does not judge food safety.
