Overlay Camera

Overlay Camera is an iOS app that shows a photo you choose as a translucent guide on the camera screen, so you can align your composition and take a new photo in the same framing.

  1. Choose a guide/reference photo
  2. Show it as a translucent guide on the camera screen
  3. Take a new photo while aligning the composition
  4. Compare the guide photo and captured photo with the comparison slider
