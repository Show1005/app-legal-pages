Overlay Camera Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Overlay Camera" handles user information.

1. Camera Access

This app accesses the camera to take photos.

Photos taken with the camera are not used for any purpose other than taking the photo.

2. Photo Library Access

This app accesses your photo library so you can choose a guide/reference photo to align your composition with.

The selected guide/reference photo is used only to display it as a translucent guide on the camera screen.

3. Data Stored on This Device

This app does not collect personal information.

The selected guide/reference photo and the captured photo are stored only inside this app's own container on your device.

The guide/reference photo is not composited into the captured photo. They are stored as separate data.

4. Data Transmission

Data saved in this app is not sent to the developer's server.

This app has no backend server and does not communicate with any external API.

Your data is managed only on your own device.

5. Accounts and Third-Party Services

This app does not use any of the following:

  • Account creation or login
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Paid features or in-app purchases (in this first release)
  • Analytics SDKs
  • Third-party tracking tools
  • Cloud sync

6. Data Deletion

When you delete a session (a guide/reference photo and captured photo pair) inside the app, the related images and metadata are deleted from your device.

If you delete the app itself, all data stored on your device is also deleted.

8. Changes
