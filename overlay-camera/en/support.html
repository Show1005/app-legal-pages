Overlay Camera Support

"Overlay Camera" is a camera app that shows a guide/reference photo as a translucent overlay so you can align your composition and take a new photo in the same framing.

Basic Usage

  1. Choose a guide/reference photo.
  2. It appears as a translucent guide on the camera screen.
  3. Take a new photo while aligning your composition with the guide.
  4. Compare the guide photo and the captured photo with the comparison slider.

FAQ

  1. Is the guide/reference photo included in the saved captured photo?

    No. The guide/reference photo is not composited into the captured photo — they are stored as separate data.

  2. Are photos saved to the Photos app?

    No. In this first release, photos are stored only inside the app, not in your device's Photos app.

  3. Is data sent outside the device?

    No. This app has no backend server or external API, and all data is stored on your device.

  4. What happens when I delete a session?

    The guide/reference photo, captured photo, and their metadata for that session are deleted from your device.

  5. Do I need to create an account?

    No. This app can be used without creating an account or signing in.

  6. Are there ads or paid features?

    No. This first release has no advertising and no paid features.
