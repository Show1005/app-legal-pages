Direction Compass Support

"Direction Compass" is an iOS app that uses your current location and device heading to show only the direction toward a destination or selected compass direction with an arrow. It does not provide maps, route guidance, or navigation.

What You Can Do

  • Set one destination and show its direction with an arrow
  • Calculate the direction to a destination from your current location and device heading
  • Show north, east, south, and west
  • Show the lucky direction for each year

Basic Usage

  1. Enter a destination name, latitude, and longitude in Settings.
  2. Check the arrow direction on the main screen.
  3. North, east, south, west, and lucky direction modes can be used even without a destination.

Notes

  • Heading may be unstable indoors or in places with strong magnetic interference.
  • This app does not provide maps, route guidance, or navigation.
  • If location permission is not allowed, the app cannot calculate the direction to a destination.
  • North, east, south, west, and lucky direction modes use the device heading.

FAQ

  1. The direction to my destination is not shown.

    Please check that location access is allowed in the Settings app and that the destination latitude and longitude are entered correctly.

  2. The arrow direction seems incorrect.

    Device heading can become unstable indoors or in places with strong magnetic interference. Please try again outdoors with a clear view.

  3. Can I use the app without setting a destination?

    Yes. North, east, south, west, and lucky direction modes can be used with only the device heading.

  4. Can I check routes on a map?

    No. This app does not provide map display or route guidance. It only shows the direction to face.

  5. Are my destination and location data sent outside the device?

    No. Location and destination information are used only on your device and are not sent to a server.
