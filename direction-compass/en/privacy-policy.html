Direction Compass Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Direction Compass" handles user information.

Direction Compass shows the direction toward a destination or selected compass direction with an arrow. It does not provide maps, route guidance, or navigation.

1. Information This App May Handle

This app does not collect personal information. The following information entered in the app is stored only on the user's device.

  • Location information (current location)
  • Device heading
  • Destination name, latitude, and longitude entered by the user

2. Purpose of Use

The app uses this information only to calculate the bearing from your current location to a destination and to display directions such as north, east, south, west, and the lucky direction.

Location information and device heading are used only for direction calculation and are not used for other purposes.

3. Data Storage

Destination name, latitude, and longitude entered by the user are stored only on the user's device.

Location information and device heading are used only for direction calculation and are not stored.

4. Data Transmission

Location information and destination information are not sent to the developer's server or any other external server.

This information is processed only on the user's device.

5. Third-Party Sharing

This app does not provide collected information to third parties.

6. Advertising

This app does not display advertising and does not use advertising SDKs.

7. Analytics

This app does not use analytics SDKs or other tracking tools.

8. Accounts and Login

This app does not use account registration or login features.
