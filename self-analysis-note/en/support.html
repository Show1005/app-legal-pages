Self Analysis Note Support

"Self Analysis Note" is an app for organizing your career history, experiences, interests, and strengths, then reviewing and copying them as a self-analysis memo.

What You Can Do

  • Add, edit, and delete career timeline entries
  • Add, edit, and delete experience notes
  • List certifications, interests, strengths, hobbies, and values
  • Review and copy a self-analysis memo from registered content
  • Delete all local data

Data storage

Information you enter is stored on your device. The app does not use accounts, login, cloud sync, or external API integrations.

You can delete saved information from each edit screen or from Settings.

Self-analysis memo

The self-analysis memo lets you review registered content as Markdown-style text and copy it to the clipboard through a user action.

You can review the content before copying. The app does not automatically detect or anonymize personal information.

Privacy Policy

Please review the Privacy Policy.

FAQ

  1. Is my data sent to the developer's server?

    No. Data is not sent to the developer's server.

  2. What happens if I delete the app?

    Data stored locally on your device is also deleted.

  3. Is the self-analysis memo sent automatically?

    No. It is never sent automatically. Copying is performed manually by the user.
