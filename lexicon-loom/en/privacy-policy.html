Lexicon Loom Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Lexicon Loom" handles user information.

Lexicon Loom lets you organize items made of a keyword and its content into categories, building your own personal dictionary and knowledge notebook.

1. Information We Handle

This app may handle the following information:

  • Items you register (keyword and content) and categories (name, hierarchy, order and category type)
  • Checklist completion states, counter values, and quiz correct/incorrect records
  • Images you attach to items (selected by you from your photo library or Files)
  • Backups you create, and backup or CSV data you paste or import
  • Browsing history and favorites in the in-app browser
  • Unsaved drafts, display settings such as dark mode, and a record of whether the start guide has been shown

2. Purpose of Use

This information is used only for the following purposes:

  • To display, search, edit, reorder and delete items and categories
  • To provide the checklist, counter and quiz features
  • To create and restore backups, and to bulk import or export data as CSV
  • To export lists as PDF or text when you choose to
  • To keep your settings and drafts for later use

3. Photos and Files

  • Only the photos you select in the picker are read, when you attach an image to an item.
  • Only the files (images, backups, CSV, etc.) you select in the file picker are read.
  • The app does not access your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

4. Data Storage

  • Items, categories, attached images and backups are stored only on your device.
  • The app does not sync with iCloud or any other cloud service.
  • Up to the three most recent backups are kept on your device automatically.
  • Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. Please export a backup beforehand if needed.

5. External Transmission

This app does not use its own server and does not send the information it handles to the developer's server or any other external server.

However, depending on your actions, the app may communicate with external services as follows:

  • When you choose Google Search, Wikipedia or ChatGPT on an item's detail screen, that item's keyword is sent to the service as a search term.
  • When you open a website in the in-app browser or from a link in an item, the app communicates with that website.
  • You choose where exported files and copied text are sent or saved.

How these external services handle information is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

This app does not provide collected information to third parties.

7. Advertising

This app does not display ads and does not use any advertising SDK.

8. Analytics

This app does not use any analytics SDK or other tracking tools.

9. Accounts and Login

This app does not use account registration or login.
