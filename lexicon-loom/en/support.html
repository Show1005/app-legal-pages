Lexicon Loom Support

"Lexicon Loom" is an iOS app that lets you organize items made of a keyword and its content into up to three levels of categories, building your own personal dictionary and knowledge notebook. Vocabulary lists, flashcards, checklists and quick notes all fit in one app.

Basic Usage

  1. Enter a keyword and content in the input area at the bottom of the home screen, then tap "+" at the bottom right to add an item.
  2. Tap "+" at the top right to add a category. Categories can be nested up to three levels.
  3. Swipe an item or category left for "Edit" and "Delete", or right for "Move".
  4. Long-press and drag to change the order.
  5. Double-tap an item to open its detail screen, where you can view the full text, copy it, or search it externally.
  6. Use the magnifier icon at the top to search across categories, keywords and content.

Key Features

  • Category types: switch between normal, checklist, counter and quiz
  • Quiz mode: questions in random order, most-missed first and more, with correct/incorrect records
  • Input from browser: register text selected in the in-app browser
  • Bulk input and export: import and export as CSV, and export lists as PDF or text
  • Image attachments: attach images to items
  • Backups: keep the three most recent backups on your device, and export them as text to move to another device
  • Dark mode: switch it in Settings

Notes

  • Data is stored only on your device and is not synced to the cloud. Deleting the app also deletes your data, so regular backups are recommended.
  • Deleting an item takes effect immediately. If you delete something by mistake, restore it from a backup.
  • Choosing Google Search, Wikipedia or ChatGPT sends the keyword to that service.

FAQ

  1. How do I move my data to a new device?

    On the old device, go to Settings → Data Backup → Create New, then copy the text from "View". On the new device, paste it into "Input Backup", save it, and run "Restore".

  2. How do I add many items at once with CSV?

    Open the menu (≡) on the home screen, choose "Bulk Input", and paste one item per line as keyword,content. Wrap a field in double quotes to include line breaks. The delimiter can be changed in the advanced settings.

  3. How do I use quiz mode?

    Set a category's type to Quiz with "Change Category Type" in the menu, then choose "Quiz Mode" from the menu in that category.

  4. The text I was typing disappeared.

    Unsaved text is kept as a draft. Saving with an empty input field also clears the draft.

  5. How can I see the instructions again?

    Open Settings → "Start Guide" or "User Manual" at any time.

  6. Is my data sent outside the device?

    No. Data is stored only on your device and is not sent to the developer's server. The app communicates with an external service only when you use external search or the browser.
