Madori Kagu Support

"Madori Kagu" (間取り家具) is an iOS app that lets you import a floor plan, match its scale, and arrange rooms and furniture at their actual size. Check whether furniture fits and whether there is enough clearance before moving or rearranging.

Basic Usage

  1. Tap "+" in the layout list to create a new layout.
  2. From "Plan" in the bottom bar, import a floor plan from Photos, Files (image or PDF) or a camera scan.
  3. Use "Set Scale" to mark two points whose real distance you know on the plan, then enter that distance.
  4. Pick common furniture from "Furniture" to place it. Register your own furniture with its name, width, depth and icon from "Register New Furniture".
  5. Drag furniture to move it, and rotate it with the round handle or two fingers.
  6. Check overlaps and the clearance to walls and other furniture.
  7. Use the share button at the top right to export an image (PNG) or data (JSON).

Even without a floor plan, you can add a room from "Room" by entering its width and depth or choosing a tatami size, and place furniture in it.

Key Features

  • Scale matching: place two points with a magnifier and enter the real distance
  • Furniture placement: beds, sofas, desks and more, shown as top-down silhouettes
  • Snapping: furniture snaps to walls and other furniture edges, with guide lines
  • Overlaps and clearances: overlaps shown with red dashed lines, clearances with dimension lines
  • Measure: check the distance between two points on the plan
  • Undo: undo edits with the undo button or by shaking the device

Notes

  • Distances on the plan are treated as actual size only after you set the scale. Until then, "Scale not set" is shown.
  • Scale accuracy depends on the imported plan and on the points and distance you enter. Please also check real measurements before buying or moving furniture.
  • Furniture drawings are for appearance only. Overlaps, clearances and snapping are always based on the width × depth rectangle, even for furniture drawn as a circle.
  • Layouts and registered furniture are stored only on your device and are not synced to the cloud.

FAQ

  1. Can I import a PDF floor plan?

    Yes. Choose a PDF from "Plan" → "Choose from Files". Only the first page is imported.

  2. How do I reset the scale?

    You can set the scale again at any time from "Plan" → "Set Scale".

  3. How do I delete registered furniture?

    Long-press the item under "Registered Furniture" in "Furniture". Furniture already placed in a layout is not removed.

  4. How do I move a layout to another device?

    Use the share button → "Export Data (JSON)", then on the other device tap "+" in the layout list → "Import from Data (JSON)". It is imported as a separate layout.

  5. How can I see the instructions again?

    Tap "?" at the top left of the layout list → "How to Use". In the editor, open "Settings" → "How to Use".

  6. Are my floor plans or layouts sent outside the device?

    No. Data is stored only on your device and is not sent to the developer's server. It is exported only to the destination you choose when you share.
