Madori Kagu Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Madori Kagu" (間取り家具, floor plan furniture layout) handles user information.

Madori Kagu lets you import a floor plan, match its scale, and arrange rooms and furniture at their actual size to check your layout.

1. Information We Handle

This app may handle the following information:

  • Floor plan images you import (photos you pick from your photo library, images or PDFs you pick from Files, and documents you scan with the camera)
  • Layout content (layout names, room and furniture names, sizes, positions and angles, the plan's scale, display settings, etc.)
  • Furniture you register (name, width, depth and icon)
  • Layout data you import (JSON files)
  • A record of whether the first-run guide has been shown

2. Purpose of Use

This information is used only for the following purposes:

  • To display the floor plan, set its scale, and place furniture at actual size
  • To calculate and show furniture overlaps, clearances and distances
  • To save your layouts and registered furniture for later use
  • To export a layout as an image (PNG) or data (JSON) when you choose to
  • To show the first-run guide only once

3. Camera, Photos and Files

  • The camera is used only when you choose "Scan with Camera", to capture and import a floor plan.
  • Only the photos you select in the system photo picker are read. The app does not access your entire photo library.
  • Only the files (images, PDFs, JSON) you select in the file picker are read. For PDFs, only the first page is used.

4. Data Storage

  • Imported floor plans (as downscaled images), layout content and registered furniture are stored only on your device.
  • The app does not sync with iCloud or any other cloud service.
  • You can delete a layout from the layout list. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.

5. External Transmission

This app does not use its own server and does not send the information it handles to the developer's server or any other external server.

Images (PNG) and data (JSON) are exported through the iOS share sheet only when you choose to share. You choose where exported files are sent or saved, and their handling afterwards is subject to the terms of that destination service.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

This app does not provide collected information to third parties.

7. Advertising

This app does not display ads and does not use any advertising SDK.

8. Analytics

This app does not use any analytics SDK or other tracking tools.

9. Accounts and Login

This app does not use account registration or login.
